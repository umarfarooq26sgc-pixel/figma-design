Contractors

Setting up your own limited company can be an extremely tax-efficient way of working. As a director, you can maximise your income through a mixture of legitimate business expenses, salary and dividends, giving you added control and flexibility over your finances.

As a contractor, when operating your own limited company it’s important to get proper expert advice around IR35. IR35 is a complex piece of tax law and there are many factors to take into account when determining your status. We will provide you with professional expert advice so you stay fully compliant with the rules and regulations giving you total peace of mind.
